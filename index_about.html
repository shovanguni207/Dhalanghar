About Dhalan Ghar

A rest stop above the clouds, on the road to Nuwakot.

DhalanGhar Agro Resort is much more than a resort because it is a testament to the rich culture of the place.

1996 (B.S. 2053) – Founding Year:
The Guni family built the first-ever Dhalan (concrete) house in this region. This structure gained popularity to the point where the whole place started getting referred to as "DhalanGhar".

First Gathering Spot:
In the early days, the building served as a warehouse for the Guni Family Wholesale Store and the restaurant of Rit Bahadur Gurung in this locality. It became the hub of economic and social activities of the community.

Adaptation to Change (2000-2001/B.S. 2057-58):
Keeping in view the socio-political situation of the country at the time, the business premises were rented out but the spirit remained the same.

Beginning of the Culture of Hospitality (2008/ B.S. 2065):
With time, the building expanded to include another floor and opened "Sunkhani Village Rest Camp & Nepali Bhancha Ghar", which set the foundation of authentic food and accommodation service.

The New Era (2018 / B.S. 2075 – Till Date):
In recognition of their history, the name was officially changed to "DhalanGhar Agro Resort". DhalanGhar Agro Resort is now a place where local culture, organic farming, and contemporary comforts come together within the serene environment of Shivapuri. We welcome you to be part of our story.
