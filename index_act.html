If You Need One

A Day, Loosely

Not a schedule, just how most days here tend to go.

  • 6:00 AM

    Rooftop, before it burns off

    The cloud-sea is usually at its best right after sunrise.

  • 8:30 AM

    A walk through the roses

    Down one terrace, up another, back in time for breakfast.

  • 12:30 PM

    Lunch on the hillside

    Or on the porch, if a terrace picnic feels like too much effort.

  • 3:00 PM

    Table tennis, chess, or ludo

    Whichever one still has all its pieces.

  • 6:30 PM

    Sunset, then dinne

    Back up top as the light drops, then down to the Cloud Table before the cold sets in.

  • 9:00 PM

    Quiet

    No streetlights up here, just the ridge, and whatever's in the sky.
